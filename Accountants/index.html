FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you are looking for? Send us a message and we will get back to you within one business day.

How much does it cost to work with you?

Our plans start at $149 per month. After a free consultation we give you a fixed quote, so you know exactly what you will pay every month with no hourly billing.

My books are months behind. Can you still help?

Absolutely. Catch-up bookkeeping is one of our most common requests. We will bring your records up to date and then keep them that way.

Do I need to be local to work with you?

No. Most of our clients work with us entirely online through our secure portal and video calls, but you are always welcome to visit our office.

Which accounting software do you support?

We work with all the major cloud accounting platforms. If you do not use one yet, we will recommend and set up the best option for your business.

How do you keep my financial data secure?

All documents are shared through an encrypted portal with two-factor authentication. Our team follows strict confidentiality and data-protection policies.

Can I switch accountants in the middle of the year?

Yes. We handle the handover with your previous accountant, so the switch is smooth at any time of year.
